Part 2: AI Technology Evolution

From Rigid Scorecards to Gradient Boosted Trees

Why machine learning algorithms fundamentally outperform traditional FICO-style linear scoring.

📐

Legacy Linear Scoring (FICO / Scorecard)

•
Strict Additive Assumption: Assumes each factor adds a fixed score, regardless of how other factors interact.
•
Blind to Non-Linear Risk: Fails to detect sharp risk jumps (e.g. credit card utilization rising from 80% to 95%).
•
Manual Binning: Requires credit analysts to manually create categories for age, salary, and debt.
🌳

Modern AI Ensembles (Gradient Boosting)

✓
Hierarchical Decision Splits: Dynamically splits data to find subtle non-linear credit risk thresholds.
✓
Cross-Feature Interactions: Automatically discovers synergies (e.g. young applicant + stable utility bill trail = Safe Borrower).
✓
Native Handling of Missing Data: Tree structures route missing bureau records without distorting coefficients.

Part 4: Capital & Provisioning

IFRS 9 Expected Credit Loss (ECL) Optimization

How higher predictive accuracy directly frees up bank regulatory capital reserves.

📊

The Three IFRS 9 Stages

1
Stage 1 (Performing): Provision = 12-Month Expected Loss ($ECL_{12M}$). Lowest capital charge.
2
Stage 2 (Significant Risk Jump): Provision jumps to Lifetime Expected Loss ($ECL_{\text{Lifetime}}$).
3
Stage 3 (Defaulted): Full loan impairment (PD = 100%).
💎

The Capital Release Mechanism

Under IFRS 9:

ECL = PD × LGD × EAD
A model with a +2.5% Gini advantage accurately separates temporary liquidity blips from true structural defaults. This prevents good loans from prematurely sliding into expensive Stage 2 lifetime provisioning.

Part 5: Responsible AI Framework

Explainable AI: Opening the Black Box with SHAP

Decomposing complex ensemble predictions into clear additive factor contributions.

🧮

Shapley Additive Explanations (SHAP)

Rooted in cooperative game theory (Nobel Prize in Economics), SHAP calculates the exact marginal contribution of each variable to a borrower's final default probability:

f(x) = φ0 + Σ φj(x)
Final Risk = Base + φUtilization + φArrears + …

📋

Compliance Advantages for Kaspi

1
Clear Adverse Action Notices: When rejecting a loan, the app displays the top 2 negative SHAP drivers (e.g. "High card limit utilization and 30-day arrears").
2
Fairness Verification: Mathematically proves that protected demographic traits have zero impact on loan decisions.
Part 5: Cautionary Case Study

Case 2: Zillow's $880M Collapse & Data Drift

What happens when autonomous machine learning models operate during macroeconomic shifts.

💥

The 2021 Zillow Offers Catastrophe

!
The Error: Zillow's automated home buying model (Zestimate) was trained on stable pre-pandemic conditions. When interest rates and material prices shifted, the algorithm kept overpaying.
!
Winner's Curse: Sellers with overvalued homes eagerly took cash offers; undervalued homes were rejected.
!
The Cost: $880M in write-downs, business unit shutdown, and 25% of company staff laid off.
📉

The Lesson for Kaspi: Covariate Shift

In credit scoring, inflation spikes or base rate hikes cause Data Drift. Borrowers with historical Debt Ratios of 30% suddenly face severe cash-flow strain.

•
Never Run Blindly: Monitor Population Stability Index (PSI) continuously.
•
Safety Tripwires: Automatically tighten approval cutoffs whenever macroeconomic volatility spikes.

Part 7: Continuous Governance

Recommendation 2: Continuous Data Drift Monitoring (PSI)

Safeguarding against macroeconomic shocks to avoid Zillow-style algorithm collapses.

📡

Population Stability Index (PSI)

Continuously tracks shifts between training cohort distributions and live applicant data:

PSI = Σ (Actuali − Expectedi) × ln( Actuali / Expectedi )
• PSI < 0.10: Stable cohort
• PSI > 0.25: Critical Drift Alert

⚡

Automated Retraining Loop

If National Bank base rates shift or currency devalues, an automated pipeline triggers retraining on recent 30-day window data with updated loss weights.

🛑

Dynamic Fail-Safe Cutoffs

During detected market turbulence, the system automatically dials the cutoff threshold back to a conservative stance until model calibration verifies.

Defense Preparation

Q&A Defense Guide: Key Committee Questions

Click each question during the examination to reveal the mathematically rigorous answer.

1. Why reject Accuracy in favor of ROC-AUC and Gini? +
Because of severe class imbalance (only 6.7% defaults). A trivial model guessing 0% default achieves 93.3% accuracy but catches zero bad loans. ROC-AUC is scale-invariant and measures true separation power across all potential decision cutoffs.
2. Why keep an econometric Logit if Boosting has higher AUC? +
For Basel II/III regulatory capital compliance and parameter monotonicity validation. Logit provides exact confidence intervals and guarantees that increasing arrears strictly increases default odds, acting as a sanity check against tree overfitting.
3. How does Kaspi prevent the Apple Card bias disaster? +
By rejecting the naive "Fairness Through Unawareness" myth. Kaspi deploys SHAP decomposition to verify that rejections are driven solely by financial indicators (utilization, arrears, КДН) and runs Disparate Impact audits across demographic segments.
4. What happens during a currency devaluation or rate hike? +
Data Drift occurs (as seen in Zillow's $880M collapse). Kaspi tracks the Population Stability Index (PSI). If PSI > 0.25, the system automatically tightens approval cutoffs and initiates automated retraining on recent 30-day cohorts.
